the market will humble you
in private
before it ever pays you
in public.
watch
closely.
everyone sees the
green screenshot.
nobody sees the
hours behind it.
the backtests.
the journals.
the trades you
didn't take.
why?
because discipline is
quiet.
it doesn't post.
it doesn't brag.
it waits for the
setup
and lets everything else go.
most traders lose themselves
chasing the
next trade.
do not
chase the market.
that is where
accounts disappear.
if you have to force a trade
just to feel something,
the market has already told you
everything.
progress does not always come with
applause.
sometimes, the clearest proof
that you've grown
is a week of
doing nothing,
and still
being right.
ORDER
THE MARKET.
don't let the market
order you.
follow for more.
