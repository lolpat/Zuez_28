[hook] Every trade has two parts: the confirmation, and the entry. Keep them separate, and every setup is easy to journal and to backtest.
[conf] Confirmation is proof that the expected move has started. There are three types.
[gap] One: the gap break. Price breaks through the gap at the level and closes beyond it, showing the level was respected.
[mss] Two: the market structure shift. Price breaks the last swing, and the direction changes.
[tl] Three: the trendline break. A candle closes beyond the trendline, and the old momentum is over.
[rule] But a confirmation only counts at a fresh key level. Anywhere else, it's just noise.
