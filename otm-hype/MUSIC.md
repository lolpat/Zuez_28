# Music for the OTM hype edits

Both videos are cut to the beat of **"Mist" by EsDeeKid & Rico Ace** (98.7 BPM, one beat = 0.608 s). Video time 0
lands on the first beat after the song's drop.

"Mist" is a commercial, copyrighted song, so it is **not** in the video files. The rendered audio holds only the beat
hits (and the voice in `haters`). Add the song in the app:

1. Upload the video in TikTok or Instagram and tap **Add sound** / **Music**.
2. Search "Mist EsDeeKid" and pick the track.
3. Move the start point to the drop (about 0:20 into the trend clip), so the first hit lines up with the first cut.
4. Set the song around 60–70% and keep "original sound" on, so the hits and voice stay audible.

Business accounts can only use the Commercial Music Library / royalty-free sounds, so "Mist" may not show up there.
