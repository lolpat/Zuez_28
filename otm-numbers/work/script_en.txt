[calm, low, thoughtful] Most traders are hunting for a winning trade. The ones who last are building a winning process.

Ask a struggling trader about their last hundred trades... and they can't tell you if their strategy even makes money.

You can't run a business on guesses. So know three numbers. Your win rate. Your average win. Your average loss.

Say you win forty percent of the time. Your average win is two R. Your average loss is one R.

Forty times two... minus sixty times one. That's plus twenty R over a hundred trades. You lose more often... and still make money.

So stop asking, will my next trade win? Start asking, does my strategy make money over a hundred trades?

One winning trade can make you feel like a genius. Only your numbers can prove your edge.

Order the market. Don't let the market order you. Follow for more.
