# Credits

"CRT PRO 0/5: Start here": a chart explainer in the OTM glowing style (9:16 Reels). Thai version; the English version is `otm-crt-pro-ep0` (same scene code).

CRT PRO is a follow-up to the OTM CRT series, based on the ideas in a Candle Range Theory video by KEEPSSY on TikTok.
The scripts, voice, charts and layout are original; nothing was copied from that video. This part: the series intro, a checklist of what the viewer can do after the 5 parts.

Market: XAUUSD (gold) only. Candles up #2962ff, down white, connected. Thai versions keep the trading terms in English on screen.
Charts: synthetic example data (work/crtpro.py). Not real market data and not a forecast.
Music: none (voice and soft sound effects only). Voice: ElevenLabs, "Patinya – Calm Thai Narrator & Explainer" (eleven_v4).
Timing: work/align.py (faster-whisper) with work/snapalign.py as the pause-based fallback.
Voice mix (work/mix.sh): de-esser, gentle compressor, static gain to -16 LUFS, limiter at -1 dBFS.
It ends on the next-part card (only part 5/5 has the Order the Market outro).
