[hook] O.C.L. The open close level.
[shape] It forms where two candles of the same colour meet: the close of the first candle is the open of the second.
[why] Price paused there for a moment before the move carried on, so it often reacts when it comes back.
[draw] To draw it, find two same-colour candles in the move, [draw2] and mark the close of the first one. Ignore the wicks.
[ex] Example on gold. [ex1] Price pushes up, [ex2] retraces to the O.C.L., [ex3] support is confirmed, [ex4] and price takes out the buy-side liquidity.
[rule] Push. Retest. Confirmation. Target.
