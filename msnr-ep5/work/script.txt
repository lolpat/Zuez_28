[hook] O.C.L. The open close level.
[shape] It's the open or close of a significant candle, a daily, session, or news candle, where a major move started.
[why] Large orders were filled at that exact price, so price often reacts when it comes back.
[draw] To draw it, find the higher-timeframe candle where the move began, [draw2] and mark its open or close, whichever sits at the origin.
[ex] Example on gold. [ex1] Price pushes up, [ex2] retraces to the higher-timeframe O.C.L., [ex3] support is confirmed, [ex4] and price takes out the buy-side liquidity.
[rule] Push. Retest. Confirmation. Target.
