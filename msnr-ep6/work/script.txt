[hook] Q.M. The Quasimodo level.
[shape] A structural reversal. In a bullish Q.M., price makes a lower low, then breaks above the last high. That's the market structure shift.
[why] The Q.M. level is the shoulder: the last swing low before that lower low.
[draw] To draw it, mark that shoulder, [draw2] and extend the line to the right.
[ex] Example on gold. [ex1] Price shifts structure, [ex2] pulls back to retest the Q.M., [ex3] reverses with momentum, [ex4] and continues higher.
[mirror] The bearish Q.M. is the mirror image.
[rule] Shift. Retest. Reversal. Continuation.
