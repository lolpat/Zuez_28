[hook] Classic V. A support level.
[shape] Price drops sharply, sweeps the liquidity below, and snaps back up just as fast, drawing a V.
[why] That sharp rejection shows aggressive buyers at the low.
[draw] To draw it, switch to the line chart, and mark the bottom of the V. [draw2] Then switch back to candles, and put the stop loss below the wick.
[ex] Example on gold. [ex1] Price sweeps the liquidity under a low, [ex2] taps the Classic V level, [ex3] reacts, [ex4] and runs up to the buy-side liquidity.
[rule] Key level. Reaction. Target.
