Format: 1080×1920, 30 fps, 40–50 s.

## A. Chart Dive (recommended)

**Device:** the chart is the explanation. **Form:** an endless zoom.

The camera dives into one candle on each slide, and that candle opens into the next slide. Every slide is rebuilt
from its own artwork in layers:
- the brush title slams in with a camera shake;
- the candles grow and the wicks sweep the CRT high and low;
- the red ✗ and green ✓ stamp in;
- the bullets type in;
- your character punches up from the bottom edge.

- **First 2 s:** the cover is already on screen (frame 0 = thumbnail). The giant "CRT" cracks in with a white flash,
  the chart behind it draws itself, and the camera rushes into the candle your character points at.
- **Beats:** cover hook → What is CRT (the sweep happens live) → valid ✗ / ✓ → Power of 3 (three candles light up one
  by one) → bullets → Time & Price (clocks tick) → Turtle Soup (a fake breakout snaps back) → Time Frame table builds
  row by row → logo end card.
- **Look:** the slides' own world: white, electric-blue brush strokes, black type, gold OTM mark. The one treatment is
  ink-brush wipes.
- **Sound:** whooshes on each dive, a hit on every title slam, ticks on the bullets and table rows, one riser into the
  logo.

## B. Beat Slam

**Device:** type carries the argument. **Form:** cut on the beat.

Hard cuts every 0.5–1 s. The key words explode full-screen between the slides:
SWEEP → CLOSE → INSIDE → TARGET → POWER OF 3 → TIME → PRICE. Each slide punches in on its key detail, with flash
frames, RGB glitch on the cuts, speed ramps and camera shake. The most aggressive of the three, made for retention.

- **First 2 s:** "CRT" fills the screen in electric blue, glitches, and cuts to the cover with your character
  pointing up.
- **Beats:** word slam ↔ slide punch-in, alternating, × 8 → "PATTERN ALONE NEVER WORKS" → logo.
- **Look:** a black-and-electric-blue cutaway world against the white slides.
- **Sound:** a hit on every cut, glitch bursts, sub drops on the big words.

## C. The Trader's Floor (bold)

**Device:** one character does the work. **Form:** one continuous take.

The 8 slides hang as giant panels in a 3D ring. The camera flies through it in a single unbroken move. As each panel
swings in front of the lens it flips forward, your character steps out of the panel in parallax and gestures at the
diagram, and the next panel rotates in.

- **First 2 s:** a fast fly-in through blue light streaks; the CRT panel slams to a stop in front of the camera.
- **Beats:** fly-in → 8 panel stops (each with one live animation: a sweep, ✗ / ✓, the 3 candles, clocks, the table)
  → the ring collapses into the OTM logo.
- **Look:** a deep-blue 3D space with the white panels as the only light.
- **Sound:** a continuous airy whoosh bed that rises across the film, and one impact on the logo.
