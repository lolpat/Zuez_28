[flip] If the line breaks, don't delete it. The retest from the other side is a trade too.
[body] And when the swings are flat, draw it body to body, not wick to wick.
[sum] Strong levels. Tight stops. Fresh flips. One refined level. A clear story. A confirmed entry.
