What you know about trading is worth more than you think.
The strange thing is, the more you learn, the less special it feels.
Reading a candle. Placing a stop. Sizing a trade.
It took you months to understand. Now it feels so obvious, you forget anyone ever struggles with it.
But knowledge isn't valued by the person who has it. It's valued by the person who doesn't.
The mistake you already fixed is the same mistake a new trader is making right now, blowing account after account.
To you, the answer is simple. To them, it could save a year, and a lot of money.
That's why traders pay for mentorship, even when the information is free online.
They're not buying information. They're buying the short cut. Someone who has already been where they are.
Not everything you know is worth selling.
But the lesson you stopped thinking was special, might be exactly what someone else is looking for.
Order the market. Don't let the market order you. Follow for more.
