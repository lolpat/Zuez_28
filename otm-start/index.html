Your future self will regret the trades
you were too scared to take more
than the losses you took.
Most traders are so afraid of being
wrong that they never give themselves the
chance to be right.
They watch charts for months,
waiting until they feel ready.
They buy another course,
add another indicator,
test another strategy,
because pressing buy feels too uncertain.
And the strange thing is,
sitting on the sidelines makes them believe
they're not risking anything.
But every month you spend waiting is
experience you'll never get back.
Imagine being five years in and realizing
you spent the whole time preparing for
trades you never had the courage to
take.
Not because your plan was wrong,
but because you were too afraid to
find out.
Of course,
the market deserves respect.
Start small,
risk little.
But there's a point where analysis becomes
an excuse,
because today is the cheapest your lessons
will ever be.
And the trader you're afraid to become
might be the one you spend years
wishing you had started.
