# Credits

"CRT PRO 5/5: The checklist": a chart explainer in the OTM glowing style (9:16 Reels). English version; the Thai version is `otm-crt-pro-ep5-th` (same scene code).

CRT PRO is a follow-up to the OTM CRT series, based on the ideas in a Candle Range Theory video by KEEPSSY on TikTok.
The scripts, voice, charts and layout are original; nothing was copied from that video. This part: the 5-step CRT checklist (HTF range, a sweep that closes back inside, MSS + FVG, entry at the CE with the stop beyond the wick, partial at 50% and target the other side); miss one step and there is no trade. Ends on the Order the Market outro.

Market: XAUUSD (gold) only. Candles up #2962ff, down white, connected. Thai versions keep the trading terms in English on screen.
Charts: synthetic example data (work/crtpro.py). Not real market data and not a forecast.
Music: none (voice and soft sound effects only). Voice: ElevenLabs, "Quentin - Narrator and Educator" (eleven_v4).
Timing: work/align.py (faster-whisper) with work/snapalign.py as the pause-based fallback.
Voice mix (work/mix.sh): de-esser, gentle compressor, static gain to -16 LUFS, limiter at -1 dBFS.
This is the last part of CRT PRO, so it ends on the Order the Market outro with the handles.
