[hook] In C.R.T., everything starts with the sweep. But not every wick is one.
[range] One higher-timeframe candle sets the range: the C.R.T. high, and the C.R.T. low.
[liq] Stops sit beyond both sides. Buy-side liquidity above, sell-side below.
[valid] A sweep is valid only when a lower-timeframe candle runs past the level, then closes back inside the range.
[out] If it closes outside, price is accepting the new level. That's not a sweep.
[none] And if neither side is taken, there's no trade.
[ex] On gold: a candle closed below the low, and kept falling. [ex2] The next day, a wick ran the low and closed back inside. That one you can trade.
