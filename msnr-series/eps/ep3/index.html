Quasimodo · Quasimodo Level
QM, the Quasimodo, and QML, the Quasimodo Level.
For a sell, price makes a high, then
a higher high, then breaks below the last
low. That break is the market structure shift.
The high before the higher high is the
QML. When price returns to it, look for sells.
For a buy, it's the mirror image:
a lower low, a break above the last
high, then buy from the QML.
