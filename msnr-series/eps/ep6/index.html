OCL
Open Close Level
OCL. The Open Close Level.
Where one candle closes and the next one
opens at the same price, draw a line.
That's the OCL.
It works on bullish candles, and on bearish candles.
When price comes back to the OCL, it
becomes a key level to trade from.
