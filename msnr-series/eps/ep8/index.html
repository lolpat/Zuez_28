CE W fifty percent.
Consequent Encroachment of the Wick.
When a candle sweeps a previous low and
leaves a long wick, measure that wick.
Its fifty percent level is the consequent encroachment,
where price often reacts.
Use it on the higher timeframe, the one-hour
or four-hour, and mirror it for highs.
