Resistance Becomes Support
RBS. Resistance Becomes Support.
Price breaks above a resistance level, then pulls
back to retest it from above.
The old resistance now holds as support, and
that retest is where you look for buys.
In a strong trend, you'll see it again
and again, each new level stepping price higher.
