Classic A. The A-Level.
Price rallies into a peak, pulls back, and
comes back up to it, drawing the shape
of an A.
In MSNR, the level sits where the bullish
candle closes and the bearish candle opens, not
at the wick.
When price returns to the A-Level, treat it
as resistance, and look for sells.
