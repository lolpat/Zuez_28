# MSNR series: Instagram Reels captions

**Ep 0, Overview:** MSNR = Malaysian Support & Resistance 🇲🇾 Exact levels from candle opens & closes, no wide zones. 8 levels in this series: Classic A, Classic V, QML, RBS, SBR, OCL, Trendline, CE W 50%. Follow so you don't miss a part 📈

**Ep 1, Classic A:** The A-Level 🔺 Price peaks, pulls back and returns to it. The level sits at the bullish close / bearish open, not the wick. Price back at the A-Level → look for sells. Save this 📌

**Ep 2, Classic V:** The V-Level 🔻 Price drops, bounces and comes back down to it. The level sits at the bearish close / bullish open. Price back at the V-Level → look for buys. Save this 📌

**Ep 3, QM / QML:** Quasimodo 🧠 High → higher high → break of the last low (MSS). The high before the higher high is your QML: sell the return. Mirror it for buys.

**Ep 4, RBS:** Resistance Becomes Support 🔁 A break above, a retest from above, buy the hold. In strong trends it steps price higher again and again.

**Ep 5, SBR:** Support Becomes Resistance 🔁 A break below, a retest from below, sell the rejection. In downtrends each broken support is the next sell level.

**Ep 6, OCL:** Open Close Level 🕯️ One candle closes and the next opens at the same price: draw the line. Works on bullish and bearish candles. A key level to trade from when price returns.

**Ep 7, Trendline:** Touch · React · Break 📐 Two swing points draw the line, the third touch is the reaction. A break can move fast. Combine it with a key level for the strongest setups.

**Ep 8, CE W 50%:** Consequent Encroachment of the Wick 🎯 A sweep leaves a long wick: measure it, and its 50% is where price often reacts. Use it on 1H / 4H, and mirror it for highs.
