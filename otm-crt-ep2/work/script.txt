[hook] The sweep is not the signal. The close is.
[setup] Here's a 4-hour range, with the CRT low marked. [dip] Price drops under the low. Two things can happen next.
[a] A: the candle closes back inside the range. [areal] That's a real sweep. The stops are taken, and price is back in the box.
[b] B: the candle closes below the low. [bfake] That's not a sweep. That's price accepting lower prices, [bstop] and buying it is how your stop gets taken.
[c] And if price never takes either side? [cno] No sweep, no trade. Wait for the next candle.
[rule] So the rule is simple. [rule2] No close back inside, no CRT.
