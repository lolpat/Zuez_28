[hook] An entry is the exact price and moment you go from watching, to holding a position.
[rule] The core rule: only trade fresh key levels. A used level is not a valid entry.
[single] If only one key level on X3 is fresh, place a limit order right on it.
[none] If none of them are fresh, there's no trade today. You can relax.
[sl] With a limit order, keep a tight, fixed stop. With an X4 confirmation, put the stop beyond the previous swing.
[note] And remember: a confirmation is useless if it doesn't happen at the key level.
